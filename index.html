How to Use SpendWise

Quick guide to managing your expenses in ₹
1

Choose Transaction Type

Toggle between Expense (spending out) or Income (money earned). Categories will automatically adjust.

2

Enter Details & Amount

Type a description (e.g. Groceries, Monthly Salary), amount in ₹ (or click quick chips like +₹500), select category and date.

3

Input Validation & Live Save

Click Add Transaction. Built-in input validation prevents errors. Your total balance and visual chart update immediately.

4

Track, Filter & Export

Use search and filter pills (Expenses, Income), check the interactive Donut Chart, or export everything to CSV.

Clear all transactions?

This action will delete all recorded expenses and income from your browser's storage. You cannot undo this action.
